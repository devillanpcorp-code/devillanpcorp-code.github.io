Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

DocMuDc is a personal, locally-run application. This policy describes how it handles data obtained through its Google OAuth integration.

  • What it accesses: DocMuDc requests read-only access to YouTube Analytics data (yt-analytics.readonly) for the videos on the owner's own YouTube channel — specifically view counts, audience retention curves, and impression click-through rate.
  • What it does with that data: Retrieved data is stored locally, on the owner's own machine, and used only to display performance stats on the application's own dashboard and to suggest script/style improvements for future videos.
  • Where it's stored: All data stays in local files on the machine running the application. Nothing is uploaded to a server, shared with any third party, or used for advertising.
  • Who can access it: Only the application owner, via their own Google account authorization. No other user's data is requested or accessible.
  • Revoking access: Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party access.

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app owner via the contact information on file in the associated Google Cloud project.
